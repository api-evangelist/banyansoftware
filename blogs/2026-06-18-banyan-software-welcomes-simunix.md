Banyan Software, a global acquirer and operator of growing software businesses, is pleased to announce the acquisition of Simunix Holdings Limited, a York-based provider of advanced identity search and data verification software. The acquisition supports Simunix’s next phase of growth while preserving the product depth and customer relationships that have defined the business since its founding in 1998. Founded by John Lewis in 1998, Simunix has built a trusted reputation across both public and private sectors.
